# Fonts

These families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), downloaded from Google Fonts:

- Anton (Vernon Adams)
- Caveat (Impallari Type)
- Libre Baskerville (Impallari Type)
- VT323 (Peter Hull)
- Courier Prime (Alan Dague-Greene, Quote-Unquote Apps)

The OFL allows bundling these fonts with software, including commercial software, as long as the fonts themselves aren't sold on their own.

`UNC-Regular.ttf` is the UNC 64's own character ROM (`rom/unscii.txt`, the UNSCII glyphs, public domain), turned into a font by `scripts/unc-font.py`. Regenerate it with that script whenever the ROM changes.
